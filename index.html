Craft

Made slowly,
on purpose.

Each Meridian is assembled by a single watchmaker, from first bridge to final regulation. It takes eleven weeks. We have never found a reason to hurry.

The calibre M.01 is our own — 27 jewels, a silicon escapement, and 72 hours of reserve. Finished by hand with brushed surfaces and polished bevels that catch light only when you look for them.
